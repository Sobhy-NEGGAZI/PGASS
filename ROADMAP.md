# 2. Project Definition

## 2.1 Project Overview

PGASS (PostgreSQL Insurance Management System) is an integrated management software solution (ERP) specialized in the insurance sector.

The system aims to centralize and coordinate essential insurance business activities within a unified application.

It provides a common information foundation for managing customers, insurance products, policies, claims, payments, and operational indicators.

PGASS is conceived as a modular and extensible insurance ERP, built using open-source technologies and PostgreSQL.

The initial release will establish the core business and technical foundation of the platform.

## 2.2 Project Positioning

PGASS is:

- An integrated insurance management software.
- An independent software development initiative.
- A PostgreSQL-based application.
- A modular ERP prototype.
- A foundation for progressive functional expansion.

PGASS is not:

- An official product of an insurance company.
- An extension or replacement of an existing proprietary ERP.
- A production-certified insurance platform.
- A complete accounting, actuarial, or reinsurance solution in its initial release.

# 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives are:

1. Develop an integrated ERP specialized in insurance business management.
2. Centralize essential business information.
3. Establish consistent relationships between insurance business entities.
4. Simplify and structure operational workflows.
5. Provide a unified user interface.
6. Use PostgreSQL as the primary relational database.
7. Adopt maintainable and extensible open-source technologies.
8. Establish a reliable foundation for future functional modules.
9. Demonstrate the application through a publicly accessible web deployment.
